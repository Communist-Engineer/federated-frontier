# Contribution model

Contributions include research, code, review, compute, data rights/stewardship, experiments, verified trajectories, evaluations, security findings, governance labor, and documentation. Raw GPU-hours are only one input.

## Lifecycle

1. A job or contribution scope declares requirements, ownership/licensing, safety, acceptance, attribution, and available compensation.
2. Identity and conflicts are recorded at the assurance level the work requires.
3. Work enters review/quarantine and accumulates machine and human evidence.
4. Acceptance, rejection, discount, or escalation is explained and appealable.
5. Credit and accounting bind to immutable artifacts and reviewers.
6. Later revocation preserves history and impacts descendants.

Scientific authorship follows contribution and disciplinary standards rather than payment or compute volume. Governance voice may combine stakeholder chambers or capped representation to prevent GPU wealth, employers, or Sybils from dominating. The exact constitution is unresolved.

Use a Developer Certificate of Origin only if it matches the desired lightweight contribution assurance; use a contributor license agreement only where a specific relicensing/patent need justifies centralizing rights. Neither is selected silently.

Security reports follow [SECURITY.md](../../SECURITY.md) and may require confidential credit. Data contributions require documented rights and policy; a contributor warranty is not a substitute for provenance review.
