# Governance options

Governance is part of system safety and liveness: it decides admission, priorities, scale, promotion, release, ownership, incidents, and disputes.

| Form | Strength | Structural risk | Possible use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Foundation/nonprofit | Mission lock and grant compatibility | Donor/board concentration | Steward shared specifications and public assets |
| University consortium | Scientific legitimacy and shared facilities | Slow contracting and fragmented IP | Research program and independent replication |
| Cooperative | Contributor/member control | Capital formation and complex membership | Compute/data contributor ownership |
| Multi-stakeholder commons | Represents workers, researchers, public, partners | High decision cost and capture | Governing shared datasets/models |
| Public-benefit corporation | Faster operations and investment | Ownership still concentrated | Commercial service/operator |
| Protocol federation | Local autonomy and portable rules | Weak enforcement and plutocratic mechanisms | Interoperation after institutions mature |
| Hybrid | Separates public stewardship and operations | Boundary conflicts | Leading option for further design |

## Descriptive finding

Current decentralized efforts often decentralize production while technical integration, keys, and release remain centralized. This may be efficient early but does not constitute federated ownership. AI Alliance Project Tapestry explicitly separates decentralized contributions from centralized technical integration; Commons-Governed AI proposes stronger participatory institutions but is early evidence.

## Normative recommendation

Do not assign irrevocable ownership while the contribution and risk model is unknown. Establish a provisional multi-stakeholder charter, transparent decision log, conflict policy, independent scientific/security review, and a time-bounded constitutional design process. Separate routine technical delegation from decisions over assets, scaling, and release.

FFI-GOV-001: governance **MUST** name decision rights and appeal for model, data, compute, promotion, release, budget, and incident actions. FFI-GOV-002: material decisions **MUST** be auditable and linked to evidence/requirements.
