# Principles

1. **Knowledge before scale.** Scale follows an empirical gate.
2. **Locality by physics.** High-frequency communication stays near high-bandwidth fabrics.
3. **Verification by consequence.** Higher-impact contributions require stronger evidence and trust.
4. **Provenance by default.** Data, code, models, runs, and decisions form one auditable graph.
5. **Heterogeneity is explicit.** Scheduling and metrics preserve hardware, network, reliability, and energy differences.
6. **Open claims.** Public evidence, reproducible methods, and uncertainty labels accompany conclusions.
7. **Governance is architecture.** Ownership and decision rights receive the same rigor as protocols.
8. **Safety scales with capability.** Access, evaluation, and release controls tighten as risk increases.
9. **Upstream before duplication.** Reuse and collaborate with demonstrated projects where their governance and interfaces permit.
10. **Contradictions drive experiments.** The project tests mediations that change the structure producing an opposition.
