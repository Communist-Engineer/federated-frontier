# Definitions

- **Compute island:** a set of accelerators with sufficiently high-bandwidth, low-latency, and reliable links to run tightly coupled local parallelism.
- **Federation:** independently operated resources coordinated by shared protocols, evidence, and governance; ownership may remain plural.
- **Decentralized:** control or operation lacks one exclusive operator. The term always names which layer is decentralized.
- **Permissionless:** a worker may attempt to join without prior organizational membership. Admission to assets and job classes still follows policy.
- **Frontier:** a rolling capability envelope defined relative to leading contemporary systems across agentic, coding, mathematics, science, multimodal, long-context, tool-use, and risk evaluations—not a parameter threshold.
- **Useful compute:** computation whose output passes quality, provenance, and verification policies and advances a declared objective.
- **Pseudo-gradient/model delta:** the difference produced by local inner optimization and consumed by an outer optimizer.
- **Trust tier:** a policy bundle governing identity strength, allowed assets, job classes, verification, and promotion authority.
- **Evidence classes:** E0 speculative; E1 early evidence; E2 replicated feasibility; E3 production-demonstrated; E4 frontier-demonstrated.
- **Synthesis:** a structural transformation that changes the conditions producing an opposition; simple averaging or administrative compromise does not qualify.
