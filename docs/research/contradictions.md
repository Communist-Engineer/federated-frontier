# Contradiction matrix

Each contradiction names a material basis, binding condition, attempted mediation, residual tension, and experiment. A mediation changes the structure producing the opposition.

## Dialectical synthesis criteria

A valid synthesis creates a new relation: for example, trust-tiered work changes “open versus secure” from one admission decision into multiple contribution pathways with distinct assets, verification, and promotion. Its residual class and power differences remain objects of measurement and governance.
