# Research roadmap

## Horizon 0 — foundation (current)

Maintain evidence map, requirements, threat/contradiction analyses, federation simulator specification, and EXP-0001 preregistration. Seek independent review of the communication model and verification limits.

## Horizon 1 — two-island laboratory

Run 100M/500M experiments across Local SGD, DiLoCo, async, merge, and modular variants; emulate WAN and faults; publish negative results and G1 decision.

## Horizon 2 — small real federation

Replicate at 1B then 3–7B with multiple operators/geographies and heterogeneous hardware. Study non-IID data, staleness, optimizer state, and independent reproduction.

## Horizon 3 — rollout federation

Use a strong open-weight base for verified code/math/simulation trajectories. Quantify staleness, fraud detection, marginal learning value, and fully loaded economics.

## Horizon 4 — sparse/modular federation

Test local MoE plus task/sequence-level global routing, path composition, and branch-merge. Do not activate WAN token routing without contrary evidence.

## Horizons 5–6 — 100B+ and frontier attempt

Proceed only after G5/G6: verified intermediate-scale advantage, mature control/security/governance, independent audits, and funding. The calendar does not define readiness.

Cross-cutting publications target heterogeneous hierarchical optimization, WAN-aware routing, rollout verification, training commitments, federation scaling laws, and governance-as-architecture.
