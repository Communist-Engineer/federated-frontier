# Roadmap summary

The program advances by empirical gates rather than calendar promises.

| Horizon | Outcome | Exit evidence |
|---|---|---|
| H0 | Research and specification foundation | traceable requirements, reviewed ADRs, executable experiment 0001 |
| H1 | Two-island 100M–1B laboratory | reproducible quality/communication curves and injected-failure recovery |
| H2 | Small geographic federation, 3–7B | heterogeneous hardware and WAN evidence within an approved quality gap |
| H3 | Federated rollout network | verified trajectories produce measurable capability per dollar |
| H4 | Sparse/modular 20–40B+ research | routing and synchronization beat the dense federation baseline |
| H5 | 100B+ federation | mature provenance, recovery, safety, and economics pass independent review |
| H6 | Frontier attempt | contemporary capability, safety, and resource thresholds are met |

Detailed plans: [research](docs/roadmap/research-roadmap.md), [implementation](docs/roadmap/implementation-roadmap.md), and [frontier scaling](docs/roadmap/frontier-scaling-roadmap.md).
